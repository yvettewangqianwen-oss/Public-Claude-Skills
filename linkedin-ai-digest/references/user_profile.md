# User Profile — LinkedIn AI Digest Preferences

Customize this file to match your interests. The scoring pass uses it directly.

## Core Interests (High Priority)
- New model or product launches from AI labs
- Capability demos, benchmark results, safety research
- Funding rounds, partnerships, acquisitions
- Strategic hires that signal direction (research leads, GTM heads)
- Concrete customer/enterprise use cases with real numbers

## Not Interested In
- Generic hiring posts ("We're hiring engineers!")
- Event photo carousels ("Great time at {conference}")
- "We're excited to announce" press-release fluff with no substance
- Reposts of external news without commentary
- Employee anniversary or personal milestone posts
- Vague thought-leadership without a concrete claim

## Scoring Weights
Adjust these to change what gets recommended (must sum to 100):
- Trend Signal: 20%
- New Product/Model: 30%
- Investment Signal: 20%
- Use Case Value: 20%
- Story Uniqueness: 10%

## Recommendation Tiers
- **Must See (8+/10)**: Major launches, funding rounds, unique capability demos
- **Worth Watching (5–7/10)**: Solid updates, useful benchmarks, notable hires
- **Skip (<5/10)**: Boilerplate, low signal
